Scope: the whole Project Journal site (home pond, journal constellation, chapter, Moonlog, Firefly jar). Visitor mode: Read.
Audience: the owner, a beginner, studying in course order on laptop and phone. Job: understand how each project was built and who did what.
Constraints: no generic docs template; learning aids kept (firefly glossary popovers, system diagrams, Reflect questions, real-file links); Others glow only when present; no em or en dashes in site content; night by default, dawn by choice; reduced motion paints one still sky.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A still night by a pond. Every journal is a lantern, every chapter a star, and who did the work glows in four colours in the Moonlog. Refuses the transit-map vocabulary the first build used and the docs default of a grey sidebar beside a centred article.
OWN-WORLD: Deep indigo sky (#1a1740 to #35387c) with a slow aurora and a quiet moon; one warm light, the lit star (#ffd98a, deep amber #a85f08 at dawn); glass panes at 7% white; glow colours periwinkle (Claude), amber (You), rose (Together), mint (Others), always beside a name. Quicksand 600 for headings, Nunito for prose. Dawn is the same pond in pale lilac and peach.
STORY: The reader floats up to a lantern, reads its constellation star by star with gold behind them, checks themselves with Reflect, and opens the Moonlog to see who did what. A living sky of moths and fireflies follows their cursor or finger.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: the headline "A journal of things built, explained slowly." left of a quiet moon, gold on its last phrase, two buttons, the first lantern pane rising under it; moths drift across the sky and flutter after the pointer.
FORM: Moon Pond, chosen by the owner from four calm directions (Moon Pond, Cloud Meadow, Pressed Garden, Sunset Shore). seed: none (owner-chosen, no roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
