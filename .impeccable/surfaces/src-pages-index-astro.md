Scope: the whole Project Journal site (network map, line, chapter, timeline, glossary). Visitor mode: Read.
Audience: the owner, a beginner, studying in course order on laptop and phone. Job: understand how each project was built and who did what.
Constraints: no generic docs template; learning aids (glossary popovers, map diagrams, checks, real-file links); Others lane only when present.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every project is an MRT line and every chapter a station; who did the work runs as coloured lines down the timeline. Refuses the docs default of a grey chapter sidebar beside a centred article.
OWN-WORLD: Cool map grey ground (#f3f4f2 light, #111316 dark platform-screen night). Uniform 6px lines at 0/45/90 degrees only; white stations with ink rings; interchange capsules with black outline; pill line-badges in DTL blue (Claude), EWL green (You), NEL purple (Others), CCL orange (Loft Concept line). Overpass signage type for codes and headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for prose. Adaptation (recorded at finish review): stations are white with rings in their own line colour, because colour carries who did what on the timeline; ink rings mark only the current or "you are here" station.
STORY: The reader sees the network, boards a line, rides chapter by chapter with a door strip showing where they are, and switches lines on the timeline to see what Claude, they, or both did.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: the network diagram fills the upper viewport, Loft Concept's orange line running across with station ticks and codes LC01-LC14, the line badge and project name at its start, "You are here" ring on the last station read; below, the project list. Primary action: tapping the line or its name opens the line page.
